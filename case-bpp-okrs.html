BPP Education · Objectives & key results

Protect revenue as funded volume falls.

By moving affected employers onto paid routes and serving every learner at lower cost.

01 Objective 1

Get apprentices onboarded faster

  • Cut average time from application to programme start from six weeks to three weeks
  • Reduce the share of lead time spent on hold from 51% to under 20%
  • 90% of new learners automatically linked to their client record at application
02 Objective 2

Free Client Operations from manual admin

  • Cut time spent on booking tasks from 26 hours a week to under 8
  • Retire onboarding and booking spreadsheets, with all records held in Salesforce
03 Objective 3

Give clients confidence in their cohorts

  • Reduce client progress queries by 50%
  • 75% of active clients using live cohort visibility each month
