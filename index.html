Three starting points

Most teams bring me in at one of these moments.

“We’ve raised money. Now we need to deliver.”

You’ve got funding, ambitious growth targets and investors expecting progress.

But your product team isn’t yet equipped to turn that pressure into a clear roadmap and consistent execution.

“We need to respond to what’s changing.”

Your market is shifting. Maybe it’s regulation, new technology, new competitors or changing customer behaviour.

You know standing still isn’t an option. But with competing opportunities and limited resources, it’s not always obvious what to do next or where to focus.

“We need design and product leadership, but not a full-time hire.”

You need someone to set direction and raise the bar across design and product, but a permanent Head of Design or Head of Product doesn’t make sense yet.
