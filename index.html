When I can help

Most founders bring me in at one of these moments.

You’re entering your next phase of growth

You’ve proven there’s demand. Now expectations are higher. You need to turn early traction into sustainable growth without wasting time and runway on the wrong product bets.

You need clarity on where to focus and what will make the biggest difference.

Users aren’t getting enough value

You’re acquiring users, but conversion, activation or retention isn’t where it needs to be. People aren’t reaching the value you’ve promised, or they’re not sticking around.

You need to understand what’s driving the behaviour and what to change.

You don’t know what to fix next

There are more ideas, customer requests and opportunities than your team can pursue. With limited resources and pressure to grow, every product decision matters.

You need evidence, clear priorities and a focused plan.

How we can work together

Retainer

Ongoing Fractional Support

A senior product and design partner who works as part of your team.

I take ownership of product direction alongside your founders: setting priorities, making better product decisions and leading the initiatives that matter most. You get senior leadership and hands-on expertise without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire.

Best for: startups that want ongoing direction, delivery and momentum.

Defined deliverables

Project-Based Engagements

Solve a specific challenge or unlock a key growth opportunity.

From diagnosing why users drop off to sharpening your proposition or redesigning onboarding, each project is scoped around a clear outcome. You get evidence, prioritised problems, tested prototypes and specifications your team can build from.

Best for: teams that need senior expertise on a priority initiative, without ongoing support.

Monthly or quarterly

Advisory

Senior product guidance, without day-to-day involvement.

Think of it as a product leader in your corner. Through regular sessions we review what the numbers are telling you, pressure-test your roadmap and make confident product decisions, keeping your team focused and moving in the right direction.

Best for: founders and teams with in-house capability who want senior oversight and a sounding board.
